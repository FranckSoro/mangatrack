# MangaTrack - Product Context

## Users

**Primary users:**
- Manga, manhwa, and manhua enthusiasts who read regularly
- Ages 18-35, tech-savvy, comfortable with web applications
- Value organization and tracking of their reading progress
- Often read across multiple platforms and devices

**Secondary users:**
- Casual readers who want to remember what they've read
- People discovering new series and wanting recommendations

## Product Purpose

MangaTrack is a personal reading tracker for manga, manhwa, and manhua. Users can:
- Add series to their personal library
- Track which chapters they've read
- Rate and review series
- Organize by status (reading, completed, paused, dropped)
- Discover reading sites

**Core value proposition:** Never lose track of your reading progress again.

## Anti-references

- **Not a social network:** No public profiles, no sharing, no social features
- **Not a discovery platform:** No algorithmic recommendations, no trending lists
- **Not a reading app:** No built-in reader, no chapter hosting
- **Not a database:** No exhaustive metadata, no community editing
- **Not SaaS-cream:** Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics (navy blue, white cards, subtle gradients)
- **Not anime-focused:** This is for manga/manhwa/manhua, not anime

## Strategic Principles

1. **Personal first:** The app is for one person's use. Design for privacy and personalization.
2. **Speed over completeness:** Quick entry, quick tracking, quick access.
3. **Visual clarity:** Cover images are the primary visual anchor. Make them prominent.
4. **Progress visibility:** Reading progress should be immediately visible and satisfying.
5. **Minimal friction:** Adding a series or marking a chapter read should take seconds.

## Brand Personality

- **Tone:** Personal, efficient, slightly playful
- **Voice:** Direct, helpful, unpretentious
- **Vibe:** Like a well-organized personal bookshelf

## Technical Constraints

- Django templates (no SPA, no React)
- Tailwind CSS + DaisyUI
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Light/dark theme support
- No external dependencies beyond Tailwind/DaisyUI
